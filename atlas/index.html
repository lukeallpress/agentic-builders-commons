About the atlas

Built by the Agentic Builders Committee, a nationwide group of K-12 AI practitioners who meet monthly to show working tools and share how they build them. Organized by Agua Fria Union High School District, Arizona.

What we collect

District-level practices only. No personal names, student data, credentials, server details, or security weaknesses. Districts can correct or remove their entry any time.
